# Payminto logo construction

Payminto is a self-deployable payment core connecting customer and agent integrations with operator-selected providers and networks.
The identity should feel clear and practical to businesses, payment developers, and agent builders.
The desired associations are ownership, composability, and an understandable flow.

## Construction comparison

Two native vector constructions were compared in black at equal optical size.
The first preserves the P initial and separates its upper and lower routes with an open channel.
The second joins two incoming routes into a single outgoing route, and is retained as `routing-study.svg`.
The routing junction communicates integration but looks like a generic workflow connector and loses the product's initial.
The split P was selected for its connection to the existing identity and its more useful favicon silhouette.

The following critique is the agent's own design review, rather than external expert or participant feedback.
The identity perspective favours the split P, but recognises that letter P marks are common in payments.
The concrete collision concern is PayPal's overlapping P construction, so this direction uses one upright initial, an open horizontal channel, and no layered or slanted letters.
The operator perspective favours plain, readable typography over shields, coins, or a status dot that might imply a live connection.
The novice perspective finds the route junction easier to decode literally, while the wordmark gives the selected P its product context.
The production perspective favours two filled paths that retain an opening in monochrome and reverse.
Actual participant recall and formal trademark clearance have not been tested.

## Reproduction

The production mark uses a 48 by 48 viewBox and two filled paths.
The open horizontal gap is 4 units and the lower opening is 5 units.
The construction proof shows monochrome renders at 16, 24, 32, and 48px and a reversed example.
The 16px proof is readable but more compact, so 24px is the minimum for normal interface use.
Logo PNGs are 224 by 48, 448 by 96, 672 by 144, and 896 by 192 pixels.
Mark PNGs are 48 by 48, 96 by 96, 144 by 144, and 192 by 192 pixels.
SVG lockups contain outlined Inter 700 lettering rather than font-dependent text.
The native SVG construction follows the image-generation skill's preference for code-native vector outputs when refining a logo system.
No raster generation prompts or generated bitmap masters were used.

## Reference and provenance

[Pentagram's Mastercard case study](https://www.pentagram.com/work/mastercard) informed the practical checks for digital simplicity, monochrome reproduction, and backgrounds.
Its interlocking-circle geometry was not reused.
The Stripe newsroom identity page was reviewed as a category context reference.
PayPal's attempted brand-guideline pages were inaccessible, so that source was not treated as verified evidence.
The PayPal collision concern is a preliminary visual screening concern rather than a clearance result.
The P paths were drawn for this project.
The wordmark uses [Inter](https://rsms.me/inter/), already used by the website, and was outlined from the locally built Latin font at weight 700.
The original generated product artwork remains separate from the new vector identity.
